## Human B2B conversion playbook — verified feedback, 2026-10-09

This section records **generic product learning** without copying any real person's messages, names or email address. One respectful decline is not statistically significant proof that any one element caused a loss; treat each lesson as a hypothesis to test on future consent-eligible conversations.

1. **Evidence before registration.** If a supplier asks for proof, lead with one specific, currently open requirement linked to the original buyer or official tender source, deadline, eligibility, service scope and explicitly verified/unknown facts. A public notice is not confirmed customer interest. Do not require a profile or registration just to see the sample.
2. **Qualification before promises.** Ask for only two optional fit filters: core capabilities and target countries. Do not claim a tender matches the supplier until licensing, experience, jurisdiction and capacity are checked. Do not pitch a procurement project with little preparation time as an easy sale.
3. **One clear commercial step.** State the paid deliverable, current catalog price, any required human approval and that matching/participation is not exclusive or guaranteed. Use an optional evidence sample and a single relevant call to action; avoid long explanatory follow-up chains.
4. **Respect explicit non-advancement.** Classify a polite "not now" as `DECLINED` / terminal `LOST`. Set `next_action=close_and_move_on` and `next_action_at=NULL`; suppress all subsequent automated follow-ups and fresh prospecting in the same channel. Do not treat "keep your proposal for later" as permission to recontact. Reopen only on a **new inbound request**.
5. **Learning and attribution.** Keep aggregates by *anonymized sales pattern*, without personal or corporate identifiers: first response, request for proof, pricing question, evaluation accepted, declined, verified purchase. Distinguish marketing opens, human replies, requested evaluation, paid order and settled revenue. Never count example notices, technical success or the value of an advertised contract as our income.
6. **Small experiments, not broad spam.** Compare evidence-first vs generic pitches only in separately eligible, permission-respecting target groups; log response class, fit, time to qualification and verified settlements. After a small sample, reject tactics that yield only curiosity with no accepted evaluation or purchase. No autonomous spend, contract or price changes.
