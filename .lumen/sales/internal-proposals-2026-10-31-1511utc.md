## 1. UNCDF-00718 — tourism MSME market-linkage and procurement intelligence module

**Source:** https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/320461  
**Public facts:** 200 Uganda tourism MSMEs; platform covers procurement, bookings, sales, expenses, assets and business records; real-time data and financial-service-provider interoperability; deadline 1 Dec 2026; pre-proposal conference 10 Nov 2026.

**Possible LUMEN sub-scope only**
- Evidence-linked directory of local suppliers, tourism MSMEs, buyers and distributors.
- Supplier profiles using public business-domain evidence, location, categories, published product/service scope and last-checked date.
- RFQ intake and quote comparison with explicit missing-data flags; human review before supplier approval or any buyer/supplier contact.
- Exportable market-linkage and sourcing reports for the prime platform.

**Explicit exclusions**
- No accounting ledger, asset register, booking engine, expense management, payment wallet, financial-service-provider integration or complete MSME ERP.
- No claims of supplier verification based solely on directory presence; no private contact enrichment, cold outreach or transaction execution.

**Commercial path:** partner/subcontractor to a qualified Uganda implementation/ERP provider. A direct prime bid is not supported by current capability evidence.

**Go/no-go dependencies:** full solicitation/TDR; data model and API requirements; privacy/security and hosting requirements; local partner and vendor eligibility; delivery capacity for 200 MSMEs; published budget/payment milestones; positive gross margin. Until then: HOLD, no import, no contact, no bid.

## 2. UNDP-PHL-00981 — circular-economy supplier/buyer intelligence module

**Source:** https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/318058  
**Public facts:** circular-economy platform and solid-waste data-management application for LGUs; deadline 11 Nov 2026; pre-bid conference 19 Oct already passed.

**Possible LUMEN sub-scope only**
- Public-source mapping of recyclers, processors, collection operators, material buyers and local suppliers.
- Normalized material taxonomy and evidence-backed supply/demand listings.
- Buyer-intent and market-linkage reports with source URL, date, geography and confidence.
- Human-reviewed matching/export for the prime platform.

**Explicit exclusions**
- No end-to-end solid-waste operations, municipal field deployment, IoT/route optimization, LGU case-management system or unverified claims of material volume/quality.
- No external contact or matching commitments.

**Commercial path:** only through an established local waste-management/digital-platform prime contractor.

**Go/no-go dependencies:** complete RFP/TDR, integration scope, data access, geographic deployment, local qualifications, budget and payment milestones, named implementation partner and positive margin. Pre-bid window has passed. HOLD, no import, no contact, no bid.

## 3. UNDP-ARG-00612 — document-processing QA automation concept

**Source:** https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice/318030  
**Public facts:** digital document processing service, Argentina, 36-month term, deadline 4 Nov 2026, contact adquisicionespnud@undp.org. The attached TDR was not accessible in this run; no budget or volumes were published on the notice page.

**Possible technical workflow if TDR confirms fit**
1. Controlled document intake and file-type validation.
2. OCR and classification with confidence scores.
3. Structured field extraction with source-page/region traceability.
4. Deterministic validation, duplicate detection and exception queue.
5. Human review and correction log for low-confidence or sensitive records.
6. Export/API handoff, audit trail, retention controls and SLA reporting.

**Explicit exclusions**
- No promise of 36-month SLA, throughput, legal compliance, data residency or accuracy until the TDR and environment are known.
- No processing of confidential documents before authorization and security review.
- No claim that this is already a standard LUMEN service.

**Commercial path:** internal fit study only. It may fit a broader document-automation capability, but not yet the current LUMEN offer catalog.

**Go/no-go dependencies:** complete TDR; document types and volumes; languages; data sensitivity and lawful basis; hosting/residency; retention; OCR accuracy and human QA requirements; integrations; SLA/penalties; vendor eligibility; payment schedule; delivery team and positive margin. With the 4 Nov deadline and missing TDR, do not bid or contact.
