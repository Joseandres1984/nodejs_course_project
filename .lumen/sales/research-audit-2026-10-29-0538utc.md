## Internal, NON-SENDABLE prototype concepts

1. **Quote comparison micro-pilot (inspired by 40738872, not a client offer):** import three *provided* structured sample quotes (CSV/Excel or text-selectable PDFs), normalize currency/quantity/lead time/Incoterms, flag missing or conflicting fields, produce an audit-linked comparison spreadsheet and human-editable negotiation draft. No supplier email dispatch, no fabricated live quotes, no autonomous negotiation, no OCR claim for scanned files, no API usage billed to LUMEN. Do not attach a price/deadline or claim customer acceptance.
2. **Procurement shipment status template (generic market learning, not 40780034 active work):** fixed spreadsheet schema for supplier, PO, promised ship date, ETA, SKU, qty, unit price, currency, documentary source and discrepancy flag; calculations on synthetic test data only. No private buyer data, logistics feeds or access assumed.
3. **Wholesale RFQ scoping diagnostic (generic, not 40785152 active work):** requirements/acceptance matrix for an RFQ-only MVP *excluding* live payments, KYC, financial changes and production deploy. Only a reusable internal service outline.

These concepts may become actual proposals **only after** a genuinely current original buyer request, payment-capacity evidence, exact fit, zero-fee permitted channel, dedup and owner approval. They are not recorded as revenue opportunities or submitted to any platform.

## Commercial learning

- **Reject false freshness**: live original pages marked Closed/Completed even while indexed snippets claimed Open. Dedup by stable project ID and URL, not search snippet title.
- **Probability of cash matters more than headline budget**: payment method verified is not escrow; a low-priced full payment/KYC marketplace with 175 competing proposals has poor scope/margin fit; conversion-only RFQ rewards shift all buyer-acquisition risk to LUMEN.
- Focus discovery on **recent, funded, narrow, remote supplier/quote comparison or procurement data cleanup**, preferably with a paid first milestone, documented buyer identity, platform-authorized no-fee reply, and an acceptance test. Never promise physical factory checks, private sales estimates, paid-tool access, actual negotiated prices or past client work without evidence.

**Outcome:** 0 newly qualified buyers, 0 production imports, 0 sent proposals/bids/messages, 0 purchases/charges/claims, 0 contracts, 0 financial moves, 0 independently verified new x402 settlements, 0 first-sale notifications. This branch contains internal research only; no assertion that the whole market was exhaustively searched.
