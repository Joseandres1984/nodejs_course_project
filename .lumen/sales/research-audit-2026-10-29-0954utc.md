## Commercial learning and result

- Penalize **claimed** tasks even when the due date is still open; reject upfront stakes/deposits even when advertised as refundable.
- Penalize single-winner contests by submissions/competition, expected net after effort, service mismatch, and unverified funds.
- Recheck **original source state** because search results can falsely suggest a closed project is new/open.
- Do not equate advertised budget, client badge, escrow reference, CI success, 402 response, or x402 *buyer-funded* bounty with LUMEN revenue.
- Continue targeting explicit buyer-funded procurement/RFQ/quote comparison/supplier verification requests with clear bounded scope, verified fee-free contact, and a unique historical source URL. Owner approval remains mandatory for any external action.
